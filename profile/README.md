## 🌊 LOCATION & TRAVEL — `DESTINATION → JOURNEY → ARRIVAL`

**Paragraph 1**

This summer I had the chance to spend my holidays at the seaside with my family. We had been planning the trip for months, and when the school year finally ended, we packed our bags and set off early in the morning. The journey took almost a whole day—first by train, then by bus—but the moment we saw the sea, all the tiredness disappeared. The air smelled of salt, the seagulls were calling, and I knew the next three weeks would be unforgettable.

`DESTINATION → JOURNEY → ARRIVAL`

---

## 🏖️ DAILY ROUTINES — `MORNING → AFTERNOON → EVENING`

**Paragraph 2**

Our days followed a simple but pleasant routine. We usually woke up around eight, had a quick breakfast on the balcony, and went straight to the beach. I spent hours swimming, building sandcastles with my younger sister, and reading under an umbrella. In the afternoon, when the sun became too strong, we returned to our rented flat for lunch and a short nap. Later, we would walk along the promenade, buy ice cream, and watch the boats in the harbour.

`MORNING → AFTERNOON → EVENING`

---

## 🎢 ACTIVITIES & HIGHLIGHTS — `ADVENTURE → DISCOVERY → FUN`

**Paragraph 3**

The most exciting part of the holiday was a boat trip to a nearby island. We explored a small fishing village, tasted fresh seafood, and climbed a hill that offered a breathtaking view of the coastline. Another highlight was a local music festival we stumbled upon by chance. The streets were full of people dancing, and we stayed out late, enjoying the atmosphere and the warm night air.

`ADVENTURE → DISCOVERY → FUN`

---

## 🌅 EVENINGS & REFLECTIONS — `THOUGHTS → FEELINGS → LESSON`

**Paragraph 4**

In the evenings, we often sat on the beach and watched the sunset. Those quiet moments gave me time to think about the past year and the year ahead. I realised that holidays are not only about rest, but also about spending time with the people you love. By the time we returned home, I felt refreshed, happy, and ready for a new school year.

`THOUGHTS → FEELINGS → LESSON`
